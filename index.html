Profile

About

Dariia Pabolkova is a third-year PhD candidate in Business Intelligence and Big Data Management at the College of Business, Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) in Shenzhen, China. Her work sits between information systems, human–AI interaction and digital service design.

She studies how generative AI and data-driven platforms change the information organizations produce and the decisions people make with it. Current projects span corporate reporting, user interaction with AI-mediated services, platform design and quantitative economic history.

Her methods are data-driven throughout. Doctoral coursework in machine learning, optimization and big data analytics has given her working command of the machine-learning toolkit, from regression, tree ensembles and gradient boosting to neural networks, transformers, large language models and causal machine learning. In her own projects she pairs these with large-scale text analysis and with quasi-experimental and experimental designs. Her papers target leading international journals in information systems and management.

Current work: Organizational impact of generative AI · User reliance on AI-mediated platforms · Quantitative archival study · Game-theoretic platform design
